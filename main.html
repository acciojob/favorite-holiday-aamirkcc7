Paris, France


Paris, often called the City of Light, is famous for its rich history, stunning architecture, world-class art, and romantic atmosphere. It is a dream destination for travelers who love culture, food, and timeless beauty.

  1. Eiffel Tower – The iconic symbol of Paris
  2. Louvre Museum – Home to the Mona Lisa
  3. Notre-Dame Cathedral – A masterpiece of Gothic architecture
  4. Champs-Élysées – Famous avenue for shopping and cafes
  5. Montmartre – Artistic neighborhood with stunning views
  • Croissants – Classic French breakfast pastry
  • Crème Brûlée – Rich and creamy dessert
  • Macarons – Colorful and delicate sweets
  • Baguette – Traditional French bread
  • Cheese – A wide variety of famous French cheeses
